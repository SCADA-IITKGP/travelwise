Add approved company policy text files here. Legacy files directly in this folder belong to the `default` organization. For multiple organizations, create one subfolder per organization using a short ID (letters, numbers, dots, underscores or hyphens), for example `data/policies/acme/` and `data/policies/contoso/`.

Store approved `.md` or `.txt` policies in the organization's folder. Markdown headings, numbered or bulleted items, and blank-line separated paragraphs are indexed as individual clauses. The policy status endpoint reports clause count and whether the 500-clause target is met for that organization. Add only policies approved by the organization; the app does not generate or invent policy rules. Eligibility decisions remain unavailable unless approved policy evidence supports them.
